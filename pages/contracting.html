Crafting Spaces with Quality and Precision

Our contracting division delivers reliable construction and fit-out services with a focus on quality craftsmanship, professional project management, and on-time delivery across the UAE.

Construction

Civil & General Contracting

From foundation to finish, our civil and general contracting services cover all aspects of building construction. We manage the complete life-cycle of construction projects — residential, commercial, and industrial.

  • Structural concrete and steel works
  • Masonry and blockwork
  • Waterproofing and insulation
  • Site supervision and project management
  • Quality assurance and safety compliance
Interiors

Gypsum & Ceiling Works

We design and install precision gypsum partitions, false ceilings, and decorative mouldings that define the character of your interior spaces — delivering flawless finishes every time.

  • Gypsum board partitions and cladding
  • Suspended and false ceiling systems
  • Decorative cornices and mouldings
  • Fire-rated and acoustic partitions
  • Concealed lighting integration
Facades

Glass & Aluminum Works

From sleek glass facades to custom aluminum cladding, we create modern exterior and interior solutions that combine aesthetics with structural performance and energy efficiency.

  • Curtain wall systems
  • Aluminum doors, windows, and shopfronts
  • Glass partitions and balustrades
  • Spider and structural glazing
  • Aluminum composite panel (ACP) cladding
Finishing

Painting & Finishing

Our professional painting and finishing teams deliver flawless interior and exterior surfaces using premium materials and meticulous application techniques for long-lasting results.

  • Interior and exterior painting
  • Textured and decorative wall finishes
  • Epoxy and polyurethane coatings
  • Wallpaper installation
  • Wood staining and lacquering
Flooring

Premium Flooring Solutions

We source and install the finest flooring materials — from natural stone and marble to engineered hardwood, ceramic tiles, and industrial-grade epoxy — tailored to your project's needs.

  • Marble, granite, and natural stone

  • Epoxy and self-leveling floor coatings

Fit-Out
