Property Description

This stunning luxury 2-bedroom apartment is located in the heart of Al Reem Island, one of Abu Dhabi's most prestigious addresses. The property offers breathtaking views of the city skyline and the Arabian Gulf, with floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the living spaces with natural light.


The apartment features premium finishes throughout, including marble flooring, Italian kitchen cabinetry, and designer bathroom fixtures. The open-plan living and dining area flows seamlessly onto a spacious balcony, perfect for entertaining or relaxing while enjoying the panoramic views. Both bedrooms are generously sized with built-in wardrobes and en-suite bathrooms.


Residents enjoy access to world-class amenities including an infinity pool, state-of-the-art gymnasium, landscaped gardens, children's play area, and 24/7 security with concierge services. The building is within walking distance of The Galleria Mall, premium restaurants, and waterfront promenades.
